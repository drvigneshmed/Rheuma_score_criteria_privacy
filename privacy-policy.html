Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rheuma - Scores Criteria (the “App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the application.

The App is designed as a clinical and educational reference tool for healthcare professionals, medical students, trainees, and other users who wish to calculate or review rheumatology scoring systems and disease classification criteria.

We respect your privacy and have designed the App, where technically feasible, to operate with minimal data collection.

Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

The App does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide your name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, photograph, medical registration number, hospital ID, or Google account details. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through the App.

1.2 Patient Information Entered Into the App

The App may allow users to enter clinical findings, laboratory values, imaging findings, symptoms, examination findings, or other information needed to calculate a score or evaluate criteria. Such information is processed locally on your device where the relevant feature is designed for local processing.

Mandatory User Guidance

Users should NEVER enter directly identifiable patient information, including patient name, phone number, address, hospital/MRN number, Aadhaar, PAN, photograph, or other directly identifying information. The App does not require such information for its intended functionality.

1.3 Automatically Collected Technical Information

The App itself is designed to minimize data collection. Depending on your device, Android, Google Play services, and third-party services used by the App may process limited technical, diagnostic, crash, security, or advertising-related information in accordance with their respective policies.

Local Processing and Storage

Calculator inputs are not intentionally uploaded by the App to remote servers. We do not maintain a central database of patient information and do not create cloud patient profiles.

If the App provides a feature that allows assessments to be saved, those assessments are intended to be stored locally on your device. Locally stored assessments remain on your device until you clear, delete, or otherwise remove them, subject to Android/device storage behavior.

Advertising

The App may display banner advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When advertising is enabled, Google and its service providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interaction information, and advertising-related information as permitted by applicable law and your consent/settings.

Clinical calculator inputs and saved assessment content are not intentionally transmitted to advertising services by the App for the purpose of serving ads.

Advertising availability may vary depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and whether you have purchased the App’s lifetime ad-removal entitlement.

Remove Ads — Lifetime Purchase

The App may offer a one-time in-app purchase called “Remove Ads — Lifetime”. The product ID is rheuma_remove_ads_lifetime.

This purchase is processed through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card, bank account, UPI credentials, or other payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

A successful purchase may provide lifetime removal of banner advertisements within the App, subject to the functionality and terms of Google Play.

Third-Party Services

The App may rely on or interact with third-party services, including:

  • Google Play and Google Play services for app distribution and related services.
  • Google Play Billing for in-app purchase processing.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for advertising, when enabled.
  • Android operating-system services for device-level functionality, security, diagnostics, and crash reporting.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the applicable policies of those providers.

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended primarily for healthcare professionals, medical students, trainees, and other users seeking clinical or educational information. The App is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the design of the App to reduce unnecessary collection and exposure of information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for securing their own devices and for avoiding entry of directly identifiable patient information into the App.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a central database of your calculator inputs or saved assessments. If information is stored locally by an App feature, you may generally remove it using the relevant App controls or by clearing the App's local data, uninstalling the App, or using applicable Android device controls.

Information processed independently by Google Play, AdMob, Android, or other third-party services is subject to those providers’ retention and deletion practices.

Medical Disclaimer

Rheuma - Scores Criteria is a clinical and educational reference and calculation tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical judgment, diagnosis, treatment, or consultation of current authoritative guidelines and original publications.

Classification criteria are generally developed for research classification and should not automatically be treated as diagnostic criteria. Scores and calculators may have limitations, require appropriate clinical context, and may not apply to every patient.

Users remain solely responsible for verifying inputs, interpreting results, selecting appropriate clinical actions, and consulting current authoritative medical resources.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, applicable services, legal requirements, or our practices. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Continued use of the App after an updated policy becomes available constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.
